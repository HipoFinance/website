question: 'What happens if a validator underperforms?'
---

If a validator underperforms, their collateral may be used according to Hipo’s protocol rules.

A validator borrowing GRAM from Hipo has to put up the potential penalty and that round’s reward before it can take part. The intent is that a slashing penalty is paid out of the validator’s own collateral rather than out of staked GRAM.

This helps protect stakers and encourages validators to operate reliably.
