section: 'validators-and-staking-marketplace'
question: 'What happens if a validator underperforms?'
---

Before a validator can borrow staked GRAM for a validation round, it must lock GRAM of its own covering the maximum slashing penalty for that round plus the reward it promised to pay.

If the validator is slashed or underperforms, the penalty is taken from that collateral rather than from staked GRAM — which is what protects stakers and gives validators a direct reason to operate reliably.
