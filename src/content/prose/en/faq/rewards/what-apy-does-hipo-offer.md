question: 'What APY does Hipo offer?'
---

APY is the estimated yearly reward rate. Hipo’s APY is not fixed and is never guaranteed: it moves with the rewards TON itself pays out, with the rates validators bid in Hipo’s marketplace and with how much GRAM is staked, so it can go up or down over time.

The live number and its history are on the [Stats page](/stats/) — that is the figure to rely on, not one published here.
