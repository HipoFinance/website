section: 'rewards'
question: 'When do my rewards start?'
---

Not instantly. Your rewards start once your deposit is working in a [validation round](#how-does-hipo-work), which can be up to about 18 hours away depending on when you stake.

After that the hGRAM exchange rate updates round by round rather than continuously: once a round has finished and its rewards have been recovered, the rate rises and your hGRAM is worth more GRAM.
