order: 5
section: 'rewards'
question: 'Does Hipo take a cut of my rewards?'
---

No. Hipo charges no management or subscription fee, and nothing is skimmed from your rewards as they accrue.

The one protocol-level fee is a governance fee on validation rewards, set by the [Hipo DAO](/docs/dao/), currently 0% — so validation rewards pass through to hGRAM holders in full. Any change would go through governance and is visible on-chain.
