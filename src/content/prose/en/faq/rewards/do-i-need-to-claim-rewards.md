section: 'rewards'
question: 'Do I need to claim rewards?'
---

No. Rewards accrue automatically into the hGRAM exchange rate every validation round — there is no separate claim step.

The amount of hGRAM in your wallet never changes on its own; what changes is how much GRAM each hGRAM is worth. That holds wherever the token sits — in your wallet, in a pool or in another protocol.
