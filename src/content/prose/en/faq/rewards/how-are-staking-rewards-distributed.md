order: 4
section: 'rewards'
question: 'How are staking rewards distributed?'
---

You do not receive separate reward payments. Rewards are folded into the value of hGRAM: the amount of hGRAM in your wallet never changes on its own, what changes is how much GRAM each hGRAM is worth.

When a validation round is settled, its rewards are split in this order:

1. The borrowing validator receives the share agreed when it [won the loan](#how-does-hipo-select-validators).
2. The governance fee on validation rewards is taken — set by the [Hipo DAO](/docs/dao/) and currently 0%.
3. Everything left returns to Hipo’s treasury, which raises the hGRAM exchange rate for every holder.
