section: 'support'
question: 'What should I do if a transaction is pending?'
---

Wait for network confirmation and check the transaction’s status in your wallet or on a TON explorer.

A Full unstake is not pending, though — it is waiting for the current validation round to end before it pays out. See [How long does unstaking take?](#how-long-does-unstaking-take).
