question: 'How do I unstake my GRAM?'
---

Open the [Unstake page](/unstake/), choose how much hGRAM you want to redeem, pick how you want it processed — **Full** or **Instant** — and confirm the transaction in your wallet. When the unstake completes, you receive GRAM back in the same wallet.
