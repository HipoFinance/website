order: 5
section: 'unstaking'
question: 'Will I continue earning rewards while waiting for unstaking?'
---

With Full, yes. Your GRAM keeps earning until the round it is committed to ends, which is why Full returns more GRAM than Instant for the same hGRAM.

With Instant there is no waiting period to earn through — you are paid out at once, at the rate that applies then.

Either way, once your GRAM is back in your wallet it is no longer earning staking rewards through Hipo.
