section: 'unstaking'
question: 'Does Hipo support instant unstaking?'
---

Yes. Instant is one of the two options you can choose when you unstake, and it is handled by Hipo itself — you do not need a DEX or any third party to get GRAM quickly.

It is processed right away rather than at the end of the round, at a slightly lower exchange rate than Full, and it succeeds as long as Hipo is holding enough free GRAM to cover your request.
