question: 'Why is instant unstaking sometimes unavailable?'
---

Instant unstaking pays you out of the GRAM Hipo is holding free at that moment, so it is limited by how much of it there is. Most of the staked GRAM is committed to validation rounds and cannot be released early.

The app shows the largest amount you can unstake instantly right now, and warns you if you enter more than that. During periods of high demand the limit can be low, or Instant may be unavailable altogether — in which case you can unstake a smaller amount instantly, or use Full for the whole balance.
