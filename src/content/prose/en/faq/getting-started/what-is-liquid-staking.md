question: 'What is liquid staking?'
---

With ordinary staking, your coins are locked in the network while they earn rewards, so you cannot use them in the meantime.

Liquid staking hands you a token in exchange for the locked stake — a liquid staking token, or LST — that represents it and can be held, sent or used while the stake keeps earning. Hipo’s LST is hGRAM. Rewards do not arrive as separate payments; they land in the LST’s exchange rate, so over time each hGRAM becomes redeemable for more GRAM than you staked.
