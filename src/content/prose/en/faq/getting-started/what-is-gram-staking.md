question: 'What is GRAM staking?'
---

GRAM staking means putting your GRAM behind TON validators — the network participants that produce blocks, keep TON running securely and earn staking rewards for doing so.

Running a validator takes hardware and a validator-sized stake. Through Hipo you need neither: your GRAM is pooled with other stakers’ and lent to validators, and you share in the rewards. See [how Hipo works](/docs/introduction/how-does-hipo-work/).
