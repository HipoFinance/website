question: 'Which wallets are supported?'
---

Hipo connects through TON Connect, so any TON wallet that supports it will work. The connect dialog lists the wallets available to you.

You can also stake from the Hipo Telegram Mini App. Multisig wallets cannot sign dapp transactions, so for those the app hands the order to your wallet app instead, which creates it as a multisig request — see [Can I stake with a multisig or cold wallet?](#can-i-stake-with-a-multisig-or-cold-wallet)
