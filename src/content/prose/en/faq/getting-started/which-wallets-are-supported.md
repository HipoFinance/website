question: 'Which wallets are supported?'
---

Hipo connects through TON Connect, so any TON wallet that supports it will work. The connect dialog lists the wallets available to you.

You can also stake from the Hipo Telegram Mini App. Multisig and cold wallets that cannot sign dapp transactions stake with a plain transfer and a text comment instead, and can unstake a part of their balance with a raw order the app builds for them — see [Can I stake with a multisig or cold wallet?](#can-i-stake-with-a-multisig-or-cold-wallet)
