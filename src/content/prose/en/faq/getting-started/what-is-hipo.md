question: 'What is Hipo?'
---

Hipo is a liquid staking protocol on TON. You stake GRAM and receive hGRAM in return — a token that represents your staked GRAM and stays usable while the GRAM keeps earning staking rewards: you can hold it, send it or use it in TON DeFi.

Hipo runs on open-source smart contracts and there is no account to create: you connect a TON wallet on the [Stake page](/stake/) and the contracts do the rest.
