question: 'How long does it take to receive hGRAM?'
---

Usually within moments of your transaction being confirmed.

When all of Hipo’s free GRAM is already committed to the current validation round, the protocol mints your hGRAM once that round is committed instead of immediately. Your deposit is held until then and the app shows how long is left. Your GRAM is never at risk while it waits.
