question: 'What does it cost to stake?'
---

Hipo takes no cut of the GRAM you stake: you receive hGRAM for the full amount.

What you do pay is TON network gas. The app attaches a small gas prepayment (currently 0.1 GRAM) on top of your deposit rather than deducting it. Gas fees are set by the TON network and move with it; only a fraction of the prepayment — on the order of a hundredth of a GRAM — is actually spent, and the unused part is returned to your wallet shortly afterwards. The app shows the exact amount before you confirm — the current figure, not an estimate published here.
