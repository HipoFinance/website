section: 'staking'
question: 'What is the minimum amount required to stake?'
---

There is no minimum. You can stake any amount of GRAM, even a fraction of one.

You only need enough to cover the amount you are staking plus a small gas prepayment (currently 0.1 GRAM) that the app attaches on top. Only a fraction of it — on the order of a hundredth of a GRAM — is actually spent; the rest is refunded to your wallet.
