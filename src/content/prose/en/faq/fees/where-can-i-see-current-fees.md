section: 'fees'
question: 'Where can I see current fees?'
---

The Hipo app shows the exact gas fee for each action before you confirm it, so you always see the current figure rather than an estimate published here.

Gas fees are set by the TON network and move with it. For staking and unstaking they are on the order of a hundredth of a GRAM, and unused gas is refunded.
