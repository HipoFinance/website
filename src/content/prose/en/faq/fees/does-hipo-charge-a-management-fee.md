section: 'fees'
question: 'Does Hipo charge a management fee?'
---

No. Hipo charges no management or subscription fee, and nothing is skimmed from your rewards as they accrue.

The protocol does have a governance fee on validation rewards, set by the [Hipo DAO](/docs/dao/). It is currently 0%, so validation rewards pass through to hGRAM holders in full. Any change would go through governance and is visible on-chain.
