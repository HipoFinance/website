order: 1
section: 'fees'
question: 'Does Hipo charge a staking fee?'
---

Hipo does not take a cut of the GRAM you stake. You receive hGRAM for the full amount.

What you do pay is a small TON network gas fee, attached on top of your deposit rather than deducted from it. Any unused gas is returned to your wallet shortly afterwards. The app shows the exact amount before you confirm.
