question: 'Can I lose my funds?'
---

Yes. No staking or DeFi protocol is risk-free, and Hipo does not guarantee returns. The risks:

- **Smart-contract risk** — a bug could affect funds, audits notwithstanding.
- **TON network risk** — Hipo depends on the TON blockchain working normally.
- **Validator underperformance** — penalties come out of the validator’s own collateral (sized to cover the maximum penalty plus the promised reward), not your stake.
- **Thin instant liquidity** — Instant unstaking draws only on Hipo’s free GRAM and is not always available; Full unstaking always settles after the current round.
- **APY variability** — rewards change and are never fixed.

DeFi use adds each protocol’s own smart-contract, liquidity and price-impact risk — see [Can I use hGRAM in DeFi?](#can-i-use-hgram-in-defi).
