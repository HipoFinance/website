question: 'Where can I verify Hipo transactions?'
---

On any TON blockchain explorer, such as <a href="https://tonviewer.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">tonviewer.com</a>. Staking, unstaking, reward distribution and every other on-chain action are public and can be checked by anyone.

The Hipo app links straight to the treasury contract on an explorer, so you can inspect the contract holding the staked GRAM without having to look up its address.
