question: 'Has Hipo been audited?'
---

Yes — four times. Hipo’s smart contracts have been through four independent audits: Quantstamp (April 2025) and ProgramCrafter (March 2024) on the v2 contracts, and TonTech and Daniil Sedov (October 2023) on v1. Every report is published in full at <a href="https://github.com/HipoFinance/audits" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">github.com/HipoFinance/audits</a>.
