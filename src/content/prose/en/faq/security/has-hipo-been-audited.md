question: 'Has Hipo been audited?'
---

Yes. Hipo’s smart contracts have been audited by Quantstamp in April 2025 and by ProgramCrafter in March 2024. Every report is published in full at <a href="https://github.com/HipoFinance/audits" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">github.com/HipoFinance/audits</a> , along with any later reviews.
