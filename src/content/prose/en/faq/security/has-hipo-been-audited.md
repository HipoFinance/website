question: 'Has Hipo been audited?'
---

Yes — four times. Hipo’s v2 smart contracts were audited by Quantstamp (April 2025) and ProgramCrafter (March 2024); the v1 contracts were audited by TonTech and Daniil Sedov (October 2023). Every report is published in full at <a href="https://github.com/HipoFinance/audits" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">github.com/HipoFinance/audits</a>.
