question: 'Can I use hGRAM in DeFi?'
---

Yes. hGRAM is a normal TON token, so you can swap it on DeDust, STON.fi, TONCO, GroypFi and swap.coffee, supply it to pools on DeDust, STON.fi and TONCO, and hold it in wallets such as TON Space and MyTonWallet — the current list is on the [DeFi page](/defi/). Each protocol adds its own smart-contract, liquidity and price-impact risk.
