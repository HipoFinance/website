question: 'Can I sell hGRAM?'
---

Yes. You can swap hGRAM for GRAM or other TON assets on the DEXs listed on the [DeFi page](/defi/), subject to available liquidity, or [unstake it through Hipo](/unstake/) to receive GRAM back. The two are different prices: a swap settles at the pool’s rate, so check the price impact; an unstake settles at the protocol’s rate.
