question: 'What benefits do HPO holders receive?'
---

Three things:

1. **A share of protocol revenue.** Revenue comes from Hipo’s staking operations. Profit shares for HPO holders are calculated after each validation round and withdrawn in Hipo Club; they are paused while the protocol fee is 0% — see [Profit sharing](/docs/profit-sharing/).
2. **A vote.** Holding HPO lets you vote on proposals in the [Hipo DAO](/docs/dao/), from the governance fee to tokenomics and treasury decisions.
3. **A Hipo Club level.** Holding the HPO you claimed in your Club wallet keeps your Club level, and a higher level earns more HPO on the hGRAM you hold each round; levels rise through the seasonal upgrade or an instant upgrade.
