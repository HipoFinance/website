question: 'What is HPO?'
---

HPO is Hipo’s governance and profit-sharing token. It is a TON jetton, separate from hGRAM — you do not need HPO to stake.

It exists to align stakers, HPO holders and validators with the protocol’s growth: holders share in Hipo’s revenue, vote on its decisions and earn more HPO through Hipo Club. See the [HPO page](/hpo/).
