question: 'Is the HPO supply fixed? What gets burned?'
---

The total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 HPO — no new tokens can be minted. On top of that, the supply shrinks over time:

1. 90% of the team tokens scheduled for release in the first vesting year were burned, and the remaining team release was extended from 24 to 48 months.
2. A share of each Hipo Club season’s unclaimed rewards is burned.

See the [tokenomics docs](/docs/hipo-tokens/hipo-governance-token-hpo/tokenomics/) for the full distribution and vesting details.
