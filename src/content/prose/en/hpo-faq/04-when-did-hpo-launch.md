question: 'When did HPO launch?'
---

The HPO TGE took place on **25th November 2024** through an Initial Liquidity Offering (ILO) — a launch directly on a decentralized exchange, open to anyone with a TON wallet, with no private fundraising round. The initial DEX price was $0.02; since listing, the price is determined by the market.
