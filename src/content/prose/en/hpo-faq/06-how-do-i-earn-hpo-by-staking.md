question: 'How do I earn HPO by staking?'
---

Join [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/): hGRAM holders earn HPO directly after every validation round, with the amount depending on how much hGRAM you hold and your club level. Levels rise as you claim and hold rewards — and reset if you sell them — so the system favors long-term participants.
