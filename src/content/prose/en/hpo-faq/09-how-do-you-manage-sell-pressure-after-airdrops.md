question: 'How do you manage sell pressure after airdrops?'
---

While the market determines whether holders sell or keep HPO, our approach focuses on:

1. Airdropping tokens to those who bring real value to the Hipo ecosystem, attracting long-term believers.
2. Rewarding holding: selling rewarded HPO resets a member’s Hipo Club level, and part of each season’s unclaimed rewards is burned.
3. Developing Hipo’s products and market, so the protocol’s value grows with its usage.
