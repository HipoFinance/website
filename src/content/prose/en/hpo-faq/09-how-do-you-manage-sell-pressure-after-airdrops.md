order: 9
question: 'How do you manage sell pressure after airdrops?'
---

While the market determines whether holders sell or keep HPO, our approach focuses on:

1. Airdropping tokens to those who bring real value to the Hipo ecosystem, attracting long-term believers.
2. Rewarding holding: selling rewarded HPO resets a member’s Hipo Club level, and unclaimed seasonal rewards are partly burned.
3. Developing Hipo’s products and market, creating long-term growth opportunities for HPO holders, and ensuring that any sell pressure becomes an opportunity for committed supporters to accumulate more HPO.
