question: 'How do I vote on Hipo proposals?'
---

Holding HPO is the voting right. Proposals and past decisions live on <a href="https://ton.vote/EQBjc5x7yY4XaB4br1n2fOfw3XwrNN5IckvkQHb4vTH8YgTv" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ton.vote</a>: connect the wallet that holds your HPO and cast your vote.

The [Hipo DAO](/docs/dao/) decides the governance fee on validation rewards, the profit-sharing parameters and treasury decisions, among other protocol matters.
