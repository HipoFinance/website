question: 'How many HPO exist, and how many have been burned?'
---

HPO has a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens, and burns permanently remove tokens from it — so the total can only move down. The live total of HPO burned so far is the counter in the [tokenomics chart](#tokenomics) on this page.

The full distribution and vesting schedule are in the [tokenomics docs](/docs/hipo-tokens/hipo-governance-token-hpo/tokenomics/).
