question: 'How do I earn HPO by staking, and when am I paid?'
---

Open [Hipo Club](/club/), log in with Telegram and link the wallet that holds your hGRAM as your Club wallet. HPO rewards accrue after every validation round on the hGRAM you hold, boosted by your Club level from 1× to 10×, and can be claimed to your Club wallet once you have 100 HPO, for a 1 GRAM fee. To keep your level, keep the HPO you claim in your Club wallet: everything claimed adds to your Level minimum. Levels rise through the seasonal upgrade or an instant upgrade.

Profit shares for HPO holders are a separate stream: they are calculated after each validation round and are paused while the protocol fee is 0% — see [Profit sharing](/docs/profit-sharing/). Both streams are tracked and claimed in [Hipo Club](/club/).
