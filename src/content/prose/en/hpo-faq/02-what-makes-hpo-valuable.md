question: 'What makes HPO valuable?'
---

1. Profit sharing: HPO holders receive a share of Hipo’s protocol revenue — calculated after every validation round and paid out at the end of each Hipo Club season.
2. Governance: holders vote on protocol upgrades, strategy, and treasury decisions.
3. Rewards: through [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/), hGRAM holders earn HPO after every validation round.
4. Club standing: holding your rewarded HPO keeps your Hipo Club level — selling it resets you to Level 1 — and a higher level earns more HPO on your hGRAM each round; levels rise through Club activity (an instant upgrade or the seasonal upgrade).
