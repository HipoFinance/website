question: 'What makes HPO valuable?'
---

1. Profit sharing: HPO holders receive a share of Hipo’s protocol revenue — calculated after every validation round; paused while the protocol fee is 0%.
2. Governance: holders vote on protocol upgrades, strategy, and treasury decisions.
3. Rewards: through [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/), hGRAM holders earn HPO after every validation round.
4. Club standing: holding the HPO you claimed in your Club wallet keeps your Hipo Club level, and a higher level earns more HPO on your hGRAM each round; levels rise through the seasonal upgrade or an instant upgrade.
