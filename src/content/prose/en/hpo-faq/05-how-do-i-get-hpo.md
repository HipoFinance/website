question: 'How do I get HPO?'
---

There are three routes:

1. **Buy HPO**: swap GRAM for HPO on a TON DEX or aggregator — STON.fi, DeDust, TONCO, GroypFi or swap.coffee. The rate comes from the pool, so check the price impact before you confirm.
2. **Earn HPO by staking**: the hGRAM you hold earns HPO after every validation round through Hipo Club — see the next question.
3. **Airdrops**: Hipo announces airdrop plans in its official channels — [Telegram](https://t.me/HipoFinance) and [X](https://x.com/hipofinance). Staking GRAM through Hipo and joining Hipo Club are what qualifies you.
