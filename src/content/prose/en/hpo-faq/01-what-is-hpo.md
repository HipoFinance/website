question: 'What is HPO?'
---

HPO is Hipo’s governance and profit-sharing token. Holders receive a share of protocol profits, vote on Hipo’s key decisions, and earn more HPO through [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/).

HPO is a TON jetton at the address <a href="https://tonviewer.com/EQDQEUr0LPi8m6D6F0Wrvuok7tZbAcr0yn2Y7hK291MMzMjM" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><code>EQDQEUr0LPi8m6D6F0Wrvuok7tZbAcr0yn2Y7hK291MMzMjM</code></a> — check the address before you swap; that is the only HPO.
