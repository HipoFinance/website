title: 'A DeFi-compatible token'
---

hGRAM is a regular token on TON, so it isn’t locked to Hipo the way a staked balance normally would be. You can hold it, transfer it, or put it to work in DeFi while it keeps earning staking rewards through its exchange rate against GRAM. The app above lists integrations Hipo currently points to; availability and terms depend on each third-party protocol.
