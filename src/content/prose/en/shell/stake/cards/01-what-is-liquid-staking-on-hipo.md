title: 'What is liquid staking on Hipo?'
---

Hipo is a liquid staking protocol on TON. Staking normally locks up your tokens and requires running or trusting validator infrastructure. Hipo removes both constraints: your GRAM is delegated to validators through the Hipo smart contract, and in return you receive **hGRAM**, a liquid token that represents your staked GRAM. You can hold, transfer, or use hGRAM across TON DeFi while your original stake keeps earning in the background.
