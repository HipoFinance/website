title: 'Base and boosted rewards'
---

Base rewards come from efficient GRAM staking and are reflected in hGRAM’s price growth against GRAM. Boosted rewards come from [Hipo Club](https://t.me/HipoFinanceBot/join): the hGRAM you hold earns HPO after every validation round, at a rate set by your Club level. Holding HPO also gives you a share of protocol revenue, paid at the end of each season. Rates vary over time — see the [Stats page](/stats/) for current and historical figures.
