title: 'Base and boosted rewards'
---

Base rewards come from efficient GRAM staking and are reflected in hGRAM’s price growth against GRAM. Holding HPO, Hipo’s governance token, can boost your rewards further depending on your Hipo Club level, and also gives you a share of protocol revenue. Rates vary over time — see the [Stats page](/stats/) for current and historical figures.
