title: 'Base and boosted rewards'
---

Base rewards come from efficient GRAM staking and are reflected in hGRAM’s price growth against GRAM. Boosted rewards come from [Hipo Club](/club/): the hGRAM you hold earns HPO after every validation round, boosted by your Club level. Holding HPO also gives you a share of protocol revenue, paused while the protocol fee is 0%. Rates vary over time — see the [Stats page](/stats/) for current and historical figures.
