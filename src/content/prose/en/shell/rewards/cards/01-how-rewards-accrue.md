order: 1
title: 'How rewards accrue'
---

Hipo distributes staking rewards every validation round, roughly every 18 hours. Rewards are not paid out as separate transfers — they are reflected directly in the exchange rate between hGRAM and GRAM, so each hGRAM gradually becomes redeemable for more GRAM. Whoever holds hGRAM at settlement time benefits from that round’s rewards, with nothing to claim manually.
