title: 'Claims and your Level minimum'
---

You can claim your boosted HPO once you have 100 HPO, for a 1 GRAM fee. Everything you claim adds to your Level minimum: keep at least that much HPO in your Club wallet and its supported liquidity pools.

If you drop below it, you have 24 hours to get back above it and nothing changes. After that your level is suspended and earns at 1.0× for up to 7 days; hold the minimum again within that time and your level returns at once. If the 7 days pass, you move to Level 1 and your old minimum is cleared.
