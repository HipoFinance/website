title: 'How Hipo Club works'
---

Hipo Club adds boosted HPO on top of your staking rewards. The hGRAM you hold in your wallet and in supported liquidity pools earns HPO every validation round, roughly every 18 hours, multiplied by your level’s boost: from 1.0× at Level 1 to 10.0× at Level 10. Your level belongs to your Telegram account, and one wallet at a time, your Club wallet, receives it.
