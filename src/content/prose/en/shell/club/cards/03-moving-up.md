title: 'Moving up a level'
---

Claim at least once in a season and stay above your Level minimum, and you move up one level when the next season starts. You can also upgrade one level at a time straight away, for a fee in GRAM paid to hipo.ton. The two stack, up to Level 10. Read more in the [Hipo Club docs](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/).
