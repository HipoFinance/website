### What is Hipo Club?

Hipo Club is a tiered system designed to reward active participants and long-term supporters of Hipo. It consists of 6 levels, each offering increasing benefits and responsibilities.

#### Key Benefits by Level

* **Claiming Discounts:** Higher levels receive greater discounts when claiming HPO rewards.
* **Sell Allowances:** Your level determines how much of your claimed HPO you are allowed to sell without penalty.

As you level up, both your claim discount and your sell allowance increase, encouraging long-term holding and deeper community involvement.

### Seasons & Rewards

Each season lasts for one quarter. At the end of each season, members are eligible to claim HPO rewards based on their XP scores.

* **Level = Claim Discount**
* **XP Score = Reward Amount**

### How to Earn XP

The primary way to earn XP is by staking GRAM through Hipo. The more you stake, the more XP you earn. Additional ways to earn XP include:

* Watching educational DeFi videos.
* Inviting friends to join Hipo Club (you earn 5% of their staking XP).

### How Leveling Works

When you claim your HPO rewards at the end of a season, you level up for the next one.

* **Claiming = Commitment to Hipo’s Growth**
* **Not Claiming = Maintain current level, but no level-up**

### Responsibilities of Club Members

Being part of Hipo Club comes with shared responsibility.

* **Don’t sell more than your allowed percentage**
* Selling beyond your allowance leads to **downgrade** and loss of current level benefits.

### Claiming Window

At the end of each season, you’ll have a **72-hour window** to claim your HPO rewards. You can choose to claim partially or in full.

* If you **don’t claim**, you stay at your current level and can try again next season.

:::note
### **Recent Updates to Hipo Club**

Hipo Club has introduced a **new model** **for Seasons 4 and onward**. The previous discount-based claiming system is now replaced with a direct reward mechanism:

1. **Direct HPO Rewards**:  Hipo Club **no longer offers discounted HPO** claims. Instead, **hGRAM holders earn HPO directly** after each validation round. Rewards depend on the amount of hGRAM held and the member’s level.
2. **Updated Level System**:  Hipo Club now includes **10 levels**, each providing a higher HPO reward coefficient for every validation round. Members can advance through:

* **Instant Upgrade**: Pay the level-up fee to increase your level immediately. 
* **Seasonal Upgrade**: Claim your earned hGRAM rewards at least once during the season. At the end of the season, your level will increase automatically based on your activity.

**Note:** If a member sells their rewarded HPO at any time, their level resets to Level 1.

3. **Removal of Sell Allowance**:  The previous sell allowance mechanism is removed. Since HPO is no longer purchased at a discount, levels are maintained by holding rewards, not by restricting sales.
4. **Reward Withdrawal**: Members can withdraw accumulated rewards anytime after reaching the **minimum threshold of 1,000 HPO**.
5. **Updated Referral Logic**: Members now earn **1% of the HPO rewards** generated by their invited friends.
:::
