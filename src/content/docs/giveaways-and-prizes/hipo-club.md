Hipo Club is an initiative created to build a strong, responsible, and informed community that shares a long-term vision for Hipo Finance.

## What is Hipo Club?

Hipo Club is a tiered system designed to reward active participants and long-term supporters of Hipo. It has **10 levels**, and your level sets the HPO reward coefficient you earn on the hGRAM you hold.

### Key Benefits by Level

- **Higher reward coefficient:** every level up increases the HPO you earn on your hGRAM for each validation round.
- **Referral share:** you earn 1% of the HPO rewards generated by the friends you invite.

## Seasons & Rewards

Each season lasts one quarter. HPO rewards accrue after every validation round and can be withdrawn at any time once your accrued balance passes the **1,000 HPO** threshold — there is no claiming window and no claim discount.

Profit shares for HPO holders are a separate stream, paid at the end of each season (see [Profit Sharing](/docs/profit-sharing/)).

## How to Earn XP

XP measures how active you are in Hipo Club. You earn it by staking GRAM through Hipo — the more you stake, the more XP — and by doing the Club's tasks in the bot: collecting the **daily reward**, answering **quizzes**, **watching Hipo's YouTube videos**, inviting friends (you earn 5% of their staking XP), and the other tasks the bot lists. XP feeds your seasonal level-up (see _How Leveling Works_).

## How Leveling Works

There are two ways to move up:

- **Instant upgrade:** pay the level-up fee and your level increases immediately.
- **Seasonal upgrade:** claim your earned rewards at least once during the season; your level then increases automatically at the end of it, based on your activity.

Selling rewarded HPO at any time resets your level to Level 1.

## Responsibilities of Club Members

Hipo Club rewards holding. There is no sell allowance: instead, selling rewarded HPO at any time resets your level to Level 1, and you climb again from there.

## Withdrawing Rewards

Rewards accrue continuously and can be withdrawn whenever your accrued balance is at least **1,000 HPO**.

:::note

## What changed in Season 4

Seasons 1–3 ran on a different model: 6 levels, discounted HPO claims at the end of each season, and a per-level sell allowance. From **Season 4** onwards Hipo Club works as described above — 10 levels, direct per-round HPO rewards, no claim discount, no sell allowance, and a level reset when rewarded HPO is sold.
:::
