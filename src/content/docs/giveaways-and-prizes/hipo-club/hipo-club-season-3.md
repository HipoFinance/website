## 🎯 Key Highlights

- 📅 **Duration:** July 10th to November 15th, 2025
- 🧾 **NFT Minting:** 612 members minted their [**Season 3 NFT**](https://getgems.io/collection/EQDtvHgDb7o_YKwSP4OmcQY4Ab4BodXFT-RWRmTvBssUYlOj)
- 🗳️ **DAO Decision:** HPO holders voted to allocate [**2,000,000 HPO**](https://t.me/HipoFinance/552) for Season 3 participants

---

## 💰 Reward Allocation

The Season 3 pool was **3,774,659.24 HPO**: the **2,000,000 HPO** allocated by DAO vote plus **1,774,659.24 HPO** (30% of unclaimed Season 2 rewards) rolled over into it.

- **85% (3,208,460 HPO)** — distributed to all Season 3 participants based on their **XP**
- **15% (566,199 HPO)** — shared equally among all **NFT minters**, regardless of XP
- The rolled-over Season 2 share went to Season 3 members who **claimed and held** their Season 2 rewards, allocated by Season 3 XP

**Unclaimed at the end of the season: 1,900,901 HPO**

- **20% (380,180.248 HPO)** was burned
- **80% (1,520,720.8 HPO)** was returned to the treasury
