### Token Utility

* **Profit Sharing:** HPO holders receive a share of the Hipo protocol’s profits, distributed periodically.
* **Exclusive Offers:** HPO holders can access special benefits within the Hipo community, including discounts and rewards.
* **Governance:** Holders can participate in governance voting, helping decide on protocol upgrades, strategic decisions, and other vital matters.

### Token Distribution

* 30%: Community (Stakers, inviters, and community members who help onboard and support new users in Hipo Chat.)
* 20%: Liquidity
* 15%: Marketing
* 20%: Team
* 2%: Advisers
* 13%: Treasury
