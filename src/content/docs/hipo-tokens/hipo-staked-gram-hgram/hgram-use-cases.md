title: 'hGRAM Use Cases'
---

hGRAM tokens offer versatile applications within the DeFi ecosystem, providing users with numerous opportunities to leverage their assets effectively. Here are some ways users can utilize their hGRAM:

- **Liquidity Provision**: Users can contribute liquidity to various DeFi protocols, allowing them to earn rewards and facilitate the smooth operation of decentralized financial markets.
- **Trading**: hGRAM tokens can be traded on Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs), enabling users to exchange them for other cryptocurrencies or assets.
- **Holding in supported wallets**: wallets such as TON Space and MyTonWallet display and move hGRAM directly.

Other uses — lending markets, collateral, stablecoin minting — depend on third-party protocols adding hGRAM support; the [DeFi page](/defi/) always lists the integrations that exist today.
