## What is HPO Token?

**HPO** is Hipo's governance and profit-sharing token, letting holders vote on protocol decisions and receive a share of protocol profits. Holding rewarded HPO also keeps a member's [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/) level — selling it resets them to Level 1 — and a higher level earns more HPO on their hGRAM each validation round; levels rise through Club activity (an instant upgrade or the seasonal upgrade).
