---
title: 'Advantages of Hipo'
---

Here are the benefits of staking your GRAM with Hipo:

- **Open Source**: Hipo is built on open-source principles, fostering transparency and allowing users to verify the integrity of the protocol's codebase. This openness encourages collaboration and innovation within the community.
- **Stake Any Amount**: there is no minimum — you can stake any amount of GRAM, even a fraction of one. You only need to cover the amount plus a small gas prepayment.
- **Earn HPO Governance Token**: By participating in the Hipo ecosystem, users can [earn HPO](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/), the Hipo governance token. HPO holders can actively participate in governance decisions, shaping the protocol's future and influencing its direction.
- **Community-Driven**: Hipo is driven by its community of users, developers, and validators. Decisions regarding protocol upgrades, feature implementations, and governance proposals are made collectively, ensuring that the platform evolves in alignment with the community's values and objectives.
- **Permissionless Validator Selection**: Hipo employs a permissionless validator selection process, allowing anyone to become a validator without needing approval from a central authority. This decentralized approach enhances the network's security and resilience, fostering a more inclusive and distributed ecosystem.
- **Competitive APY**: Hipo takes no cut of the GRAM you stake, and the [governance fee](/docs/fees-and-gas/) on validation rewards is currently 0%. Validators bid the reward rate they will pay in an auction-like model, so the protocol picks the best available rate each round. See the [current rate](/stats/).
