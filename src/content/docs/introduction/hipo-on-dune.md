## What is the Hipo Dune Dashboard?

The <a href="https://dune.com/hipofinance/stake-gram-on-ton-with-hipo-liquid-staking-with-top-apy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hipo Dune dashboard</a> is our public analytics dashboard on [Dune](https://dune.com/), a popular platform for exploring blockchain data.

Everything that happens on the TON blockchain — every stake, unstake, and token transfer — is public. Dune collects this raw blockchain data, organizes it into easy-to-read tables, and lets anyone build charts on top of it. Our dashboard uses this data to show you what is really happening inside Hipo, straight from the blockchain itself.

You don't need an account or a wallet to view it — just open the link in your browser.

## Why Dune in Addition to Hipo Stats?

We built our Dune dashboard as a **complement** to our [Hipo Stats](/docs/introduction/hipo-stats/) dashboard ([stats.hipo.finance](https://stats.hipo.finance/)) — not as a replacement. The two serve different purposes:

- **Hipo Stats** is your quick, everyday view: the current hGRAM price, TVL, APY, and other key numbers at a glance, powered by our own monitoring systems.
- **The Dune dashboard** is for blockchain-native analytics: protocol activity over time, wallet and holder trends, token flows, DeFi usage, and comparisons with the wider TON ecosystem — all built on indexed onchain data.

Because Dune reads directly from the public blockchain, it's also a transparency tool. You don't have to take our word for our numbers: the data behind every chart comes from TON itself, and anyone can inspect the queries that produce it. This makes Dune ideal for community members, researchers, and DeFi analysts who want to verify things independently or dig deeper than a summary page.

## Sharing and Building on It

One of the best parts of Dune is that it's built for sharing. You can send a link to the dashboard or any single chart to anyone, embed charts in articles or posts, and — if you're into data — even copy ("fork") our queries on Dune to build your own custom analysis of Hipo. The queries and charts behind the dashboard are open source, too: we maintain them in our <a href="https://github.com/HipoFinance/dune" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HipoFinance/dune</a> repository on GitHub, so you can review how every number is calculated or suggest improvements there.

If there's a chart or metric you'd like us to add, let us know on our social channels. Community feedback shapes what we track.
