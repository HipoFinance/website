1. **Stake GRAM**\
   Deposit your GRAM into Hipo to start earning staking rewards on TON — see the [current rate](/stats/).
2. **Get Liquid hGRAM**\
   Receive hGRAM, a liquid token that represents your staked GRAM. Its value increases as [staking rewards](/rewards/) accumulate, and you can use it across [DeFi](/defi/).
3. **Unstake When You Want**\
   Turn your hGRAM back into GRAM through Hipo. **Full** settles after the current validation round ends and gives the better rate; **Instant** pays out immediately when Hipo holds enough free GRAM. Either way you receive your stake plus the rewards it earned.

:::note
💡 _Your staked GRAM is delegated to validators through the Hipo smart contract to participate in validation rounds and generate staking rewards._
:::

## Staking, step by step

1. **Connect Your Wallet**: Start by connecting your wallet to the [Hipo App](/stake/).
2. **Enter Amount to Stake**: choose how much GRAM you want to stake. There is no minimum — any amount works, even a fraction of a GRAM.
3. **Confirm Transaction**: Once you've entered the amount, confirm the transaction on your wallet. Your GRAM will be staked, and you'll instantly receive Hipo Staked GRAM (hGRAM) tokens representing your staked amount, including staking rewards.

For the full walkthrough with screenshots, see the [staking tutorial](/docs/tutorials/staking/).
