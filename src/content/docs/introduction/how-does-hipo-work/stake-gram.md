### Staking GRAM Tokens on Hipo 

1. **Connect Your Wallet**: Start by connecting your wallet to the [Hipo App](https://hipo.finance/app/#/).
2. **Enter Amount to Stake**: Choose the amount of GRAM coins you want to stake. Hipo allows you to stake any amount, even as low as 1 GRAM.
3. **Confirm Transaction**: Once you've entered the amount, confirm the transaction on your wallet. Your GRAM will be staked, and you'll instantly receive Hipo Staked GRAM (hGRAM) tokens representing your staked amount, including staking rewards.
