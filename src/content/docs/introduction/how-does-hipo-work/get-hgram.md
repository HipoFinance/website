---
title: 'Get hGRAM'
---

By staking in Hipo, you’ll receive **hGRAM**, a liquid token that represents your staked GRAM.

After each validation round, the staking rewards are automatically reflected in the **price of hGRAM**, which gradually increases compared to GRAM. This means your rewards are embedded in the value of hGRAM.

Since it might be tricky to track how much you’ve earned, the [**Hipo App**](/rewards/) lets you see your **staking rewards and exact earnings** for each validation round.

With hGRAM, you maintain **liquidity** while your GRAM is staked, and you can use hGRAM across various [**DeFi activities**](/defi/) on TON.
