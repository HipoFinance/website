## Profit Sharing: Revenue Distribution to HPO Holders

Hipo distributes a portion of protocol revenue to **HPO holders**. Your share is tracked in [Hipo Club](/club/) and withdrawn there.

- When there is revenue to share, it is calculated after each validation round. The more HPO held, the larger the share of revenue received.
- **Paused for now:** the protocol fee is currently 0%, so no new revenue is being distributed, and there is no date for it to resume.
- Revenue earned in earlier seasons can still be withdrawn at any time, from 10 GRAM. A 0.5 GRAM fee is deducted and the rest is paid to your Club wallet as HPO bought with it. This HPO does not add to your Hipo Club Level minimum.
- Profit sharing parameters — such as revenue split and operational allocations — are determined through **DAO votes**, ensuring the model remains community-driven.
