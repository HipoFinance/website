---
title: 'Using Multiple Wallets'
description: 'How rewards follow your wallets, which one is your Hipo Club wallet, and how to change it without losing your level.'
---

Many holders keep their GRAM, hGRAM and HPO in more than one wallet. That is fine: Hipo tracks every wallet separately, and none of them stops earning because you connected another one. What changes from wallet to wallet is the boost: only your **Club wallet** earns at your Hipo Club level.

## How a wallet starts earning

Base staking rewards need no setup at all: hGRAM grows in value against GRAM every validation round, in whatever wallet holds it, connected or not.

**HPO rewards are different: Hipo has to know the wallet exists.** A wallet starts earning HPO once you have done either of these, once:

- Opened [Hipo Club](/club/) on hipo.finance with that wallet connected, **or**
- Linked it as your Club wallet.

After that the wallet keeps earning on its own balance. Nothing un-registers it: connecting a different wallet later does not stop it, and neither does disconnecting it.

:::caution
hGRAM sitting in a wallet you have **never** connected earns no HPO rewards at all, not now and not retroactively. If you hold across several wallets, open Hipo Club once with each of them.
:::

## Your Club wallet and your other wallets

- **Your Hipo Club level belongs to your Telegram account, not to a wallet.** One wallet at a time, your Club wallet, earns at your level's boost. Your other registered wallets earn at 1×.
- **Claims go through the Club wallet.** Your boosted HPO is one balance on your Telegram account, claimed from 100 HPO for a 1 GRAM fee and paid to your Club wallet. HPO a wallet earns while it is not your Club wallet stays with that wallet, and joins your account when you make it your Club wallet.
- **Rewards scale with the amount held.** Splitting hGRAM across wallets earns the same base rewards, but only the part in your Club wallet is boosted. To get your full boost, keep your hGRAM in your Club wallet or its supported liquidity pools.
- **One account per wallet.** A wallet can be the Club wallet of only one Telegram account.

## Your Level minimum lives in the Club wallet

Everything you claim adds to your **Level minimum**, and your Club wallet, together with its supported liquidity pools, has to hold at least that much HPO. HPO in your other wallets does not count.

So if you move HPO out of your Club wallet, even to another wallet of your own, it counts as below the minimum. You then have 24 hours to bring it back before your level is suspended. The full rule is in [Hipo Club](/docs/giveaways-and-prizes/hipo-club/#your-level-minimum).

## Changing your Club wallet

1. Open [Hipo Club](/club/) and log in with Telegram.
2. Choose to change your Club wallet, connect the new wallet and sign the free proof.
3. Confirm. Your level, Level minimum and unclaimed HPO stay with your account; the old wallet goes back to earning at 1×.

There is no limit on how often you change. Claiming is off for a couple of minutes after a change, and a change cannot start while one of your payments is on its way.

If the new wallet holds less HPO than your Level minimum, the 24-hour grace starts at once, so move your HPO before or right after the change.

:::note
Connecting a different wallet on the [Stake page](/stake/) does not change your Club wallet. The Club asks whether to switch, reconnect your Club wallet or decide later. Until you choose, your Club wallet keeps earning, and claims and upgrades are paused.
:::

## Quick reference

| What you do                                | Effect                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open Hipo Club with a new wallet connected | It starts earning HPO at 1×                               |
| Make it your Club wallet                   | It earns at your level's boost; its own HPO joins yours   |
| Split hGRAM across wallets                 | Only the part in your Club wallet is boosted              |
| Move claimed HPO out of your Club wallet   | Counts as below your Level minimum; 24 h to bring it back |
| Connect another wallet on the Stake page   | Your Club wallet does not change until you choose         |
| Hold hGRAM in a wallet you never connected | Earns no HPO                                              |
