It’s been an incredible two months since we launched [Hipo Gang](https://hipogang.io/), and we’re excited to share some key milestones and achievements with you. From our rapidly growing user base to innovative features that empower our community, Hipo Gang continues to thrive every day — thanks to YOU! 🙌

Here’s a look at our two-month progress and what’s next for Hipo Gang:

## Key Milestones and Metrics

Our journey so far has been nothing short of extraordinary, marked by impressive achievements that highlight the power of our growing community. Let’s take a look at some of the standout metrics driving Hipo Gang’s success:

### 1\. Total Value Locked (TVL):

**_💰 $1.48 Million and Growing_**

Our TVL continues to grow, surpassing $1.48 million as more users stake and contribute to the strength of the Hipo protocol.

### 2\. hGRAM Holders:

**_💎 +9,000 and Increasing!_**

Our hGRAM (prev. hTON) holders community is expanding rapidly, with over 9,000 members already staking and benefiting from daily rewards.

### 3\. Members Growth:

**_🚀 1.9 Million Members and counting!_**

In just two months, our Hipo Gang community has surpassed 1.9 million members. The enthusiasm has been overwhelming!

### 4\. Telegram Premium Users:

**_🏆 58K Premium Members_**

This number shows that a significant part of our community is passionate about Telegram, just like us!

### 5\. Connected Wallets:

**💳*383K Connected Wallets***

Nearly 400,000 users have connected their wallets to Hipo Gang, ready to participate in exciting airdrops, on-chain tasks, and earn rewards.

### 6\. Top Inviter:

🌟 The top inviter has brought in an astounding **115,194** new members — true dedication!

### 7\. Telegram Channel:

**_📢 +900K Subscribers_**

With over 900K subscribers, our Telegram channel continues to be one of the main hubs for updates and community engagement.

### 8\. YouTube Channel:

**_🎥 108K Subscribers_**

More than 108K subscribers tune in to our YouTube channel for the latest tutorials, updates, and insights into Hipo Gang.

### 9\. X Account:

**_🐦 +139K Followers_**

Our X account has attracted over 139K followers, staying active with real-time updates and community highlights.
