The TON ecosystem is evolving, and so is Hipo.

As the TON community increasingly embraces **GRAM** as the ecosystem’s native asset identity, Hipo is officially renaming **hTON** to **hGRAM**.

This is a name change only. The underlying technology, staking mechanics, security model, and rewards remain exactly the same.

If you’re looking to [**stake GRAM**](https://hipo.finance/), earn staking rewards, and maintain full liquidity of your assets, hGRAM continues to be your gateway to liquid staking on TON.

* * *

## Why We Are Renaming hTON to hGRAM

Since launching Hipo, our liquid staking token has been known as hTON, representing staked TON within the Hipo protocol.

However, as the ecosystem transitions toward broader adoption of the GRAM identity, we believe our liquid staking token should reflect the asset users recognize and interact with today.

The rename from **hTON** to **hGRAM** creates a clearer and more intuitive experience for both new and existing users.

The concept remains simple:

-   Stake GRAM through Hipo
-   Receive hGRAM in return
-   Continue earning staking rewards
-   Use hGRAM across DeFi applications while your assets remain staked

* * *

## What Is hGRAM?

hGRAM is Hipo’s liquid staking token.

When you stake GRAM through Hipo, you receive hGRAM representing your share of the staking pool.

Unlike traditional staking, where assets become locked and unusable, hGRAM allows users to maintain liquidity while earning staking rewards.

This means you can:

-   Earn staking rewards automatically
-   Use hGRAM in DeFi applications
-   Transfer hGRAM at any time
-   Track your staking position transparently on-chain

As staking rewards accumulate, the value of hGRAM relative to GRAM increases over time.
