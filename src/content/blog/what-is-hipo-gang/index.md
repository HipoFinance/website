Hipo Gang is a new and exciting addition to the world of Telegram mini-games, but it’s much more than just a game. It’s the first DeFi Tap-to-Earn game on TON blockchain, offering players the chance to earn real value through [HPO](/docs/hipo-tokens/hipo-governance-token-hpo/) airdrops.

This game stands out not only for its unique gameplay but also for how it integrates decentralized finance (DeFi) into the experience, making it a groundbreaking project in TON ecosystem.

## What Makes Hipo Gang Unique?

Hipo Gang stands out from the crowd by merging gaming with decentralized finance. It’s not just another Tap-to-Earn game; it’s a gateway to mastering [TON ecosystem](/blog/ton-blockchain-features/) while earning real rewards. Here’s what makes Hipo Gang truly unique.

### 1\. The First DeFi Tap-to-Earn Game on TON

Hipo Gang stands out as the first game on TON that merges the world of DeFi with a Tap-to-Earn model. Unlike other Telegram mini-games, which primarily focus on entertainment, Hipo Gang combines gaming with financial incentives.

Players earn HPO tokens by engaging in various in-game activities, making it more than just a game — it’s a gateway to understanding and mastering DeFi on TON.

### 2\. Powered by Hipo, a Liquid Staking Protocol

The backbone of Hipo Gang is the [Hipo protocol](https://hipo.finance/), a liquid staking platform on TON blockchain. This connection gives the game a solid DeFi foundation, allowing players not only to earn HPO tokens but also to use them in meaningful ways.

The HPO token, which is the governance token of the Hipo platform, can be traded, held to earn a share of the protocol’s profit, and used in governance voting. This adds layers of value and functionality that go beyond typical in-game currencies.

## How to Play Hipo Gang

Playing Hipo Gang is easy and fun. Here’s a quick guide on how to get started and make the most of your game experience.

[Watch on YouTube: Hipo Gang, How to Play and Roadmap!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37yySoET4fs)

### 1\. Tap to Earn

The core mechanic of Hipo Gang is simple: tapping. Players tap on a hippo figure in the center of the game to earn coins. Each tap reduces your energy, and once it’s depleted, you’ll need to wait for it to recharge before you can continue. The more coins you collect, the greater your chances of receiving HPO airdrops.

### 2\. Invite Friends for Additional Rewards

Hipo Gang features a robust referral system that rewards you for inviting friends to join the game. When you invite a friend, both of you receive 5,000 coins. If your friend has a premium Telegram account, both of you receive 25,000 coins instead.

This system encourages community growth and collaboration, making the game more interactive and rewarding.

### 3\. Complete Tasks to Boost Your Earnings

In addition to tapping and inviting friends, players can complete various tasks to earn even more coins.

These tasks range from simple daily activities like joining our Telegram channel, or following us on Twitter, to more complex on-chain tasks such as connecting a wallet, linking an NFT, or staking with Hipo.

Each completed task brings you closer to your goal of earning more HPO tokens and progressing faster in the game.

### 4\. Strategic Investing and Learning Opportunities

One of the standout features of Hipo Gang, which sets it apart from other games, is the upcoming strategic investing feature.

Players will soon be able to invest their earned coins in TON ecosystem, learning about different DeFi protocols and how to participate in them securely and effectively.

This educational aspect transforms Hipo Gang into a valuable tool for anyone looking to gain practical knowledge and experience in decentralized finance.

## Roadmap: What’s Next for Hipo Gang?

Hipo Gang is just getting started, and there are many exciting features planned for the future. Here’s a look at what’s coming:

-   **Phase 1:** The initial launch includes basic game features like tapping to earn coins, inviting friends, and completing tasks.
-   **Phase 2:** This phase will introduce level ratings and daily rewards, allowing players to compete on leaderboards and earn even more rewards.
-   **Phase 3:** Strategic investment features will be added, teaching players how to invest their coins in TON DeFi ecosystem to maximize their earnings.
-   **Phase 4:** On-chain infrastructure development will be integrated, along with Hipo NFTs and staking opportunities for additional points and benefits.
-   **Phase 5:** The final phase will include the Token Generation Event (TGE) for HPO.

## Why Did We Launch Hipo Gang?

Hipo Gang was launched with several key goals in mind:

-   **Innovative Engagement:** By combining gaming with DeFi, Hipo Gang attracts and engages users in a novel way.
-   **Wider Adoption:** The game lowers the entry barriers to DeFi, making it more accessible to a broader audience.
-   **Rewarding Participation:** Players earn real value through HPO tokens, which can be traded or held for profit-sharing and governance.
-   **Educational Outreach:** Hipo Gang helps users understand DeFi in a fun and practical manner, turning players into knowledgeable participants in TON ecosystem.
